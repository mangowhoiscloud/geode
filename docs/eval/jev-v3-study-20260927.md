## Findings

The 220-item intent/target panel supported its registered −5 pp
non-inferiority claim: Jev 216/220, Astra 220/220, difference −1.82 pp.
Its interval is a 40-family/source-cluster percentile bootstrap with 2,000
replicates. It is not a simultaneous interval.

Noul joint judgments had a large accuracy loss (55/216 versus 145/216).
The paired judgment-call latency study measured a median Jev−Astra
difference of −10.201 seconds across 120 pairs; this is not end-to-end
task latency or invoice savings.

The 2026-09-28 parser-corrected analysis of retained responses measured
U4 at 59/80 versus 79/80 (−25.00 pp, 95% CI [−35.23, −16.25]) and
X2 at 196/240 versus 226/240 (−12.50 pp, [−17.22, −8.04]).
All original grades, denominators, order, clusters and bootstrap seeds
remain fixed; the original results are retained separately. No new model
calls were made. The same registered rule still yields `not-supported`
for U4 and `mixed` for X2: neither establishes non-inferiority. The X2
interval is wholly below zero, so `mixed` is not evidence of equivalence.
U4 includes selection 32 + test 48 pools. X2 is transformed text-only
four-candidate step selection, not original Mind2Web task completion.

E7 completed 12 tasks × three arms × two repetitions, 72/72 strict
successes; the cascade arm made zero judge fallbacks. These observations
do not measure cascade fallback benefit or establish broad reliability.
E7 uses source5; the corrected I6 intent run uses source7 and completed
6/6 strict successes. Those are separate execution lineages. I5's 0/6 valid
strict successes and E6's 19 valid successes, two invalid cells and 15
unstarted cells remain preserved.

## E8 U8n: repaired candidates were still denied delivery

U8n's original source5 matrix retains 36 unexecuted cells after its admission
prerequisite failed. The separate source7/E8 follow-up passed U0e admission
2/2, then stopped U8n after 24 of 36 planned cells: A had 12 valid successes;
B had 11 valid delivery failures and one injection-evidence invalid cell.
The remaining 12 cells never started. The 27 registered units and the E8 plan
of 36 cells / 18 pairs / six sources remain unchanged. E8's registered primary
is `not-measurable`, with null numerator and denominator.

The separate post-hoc description includes all 11 pairs whose two arms
finished validly, from four source tasks (22 cells). It excludes the invalid
B cell and its successful A partner symmetrically. Condition denominators
are c1m0=4, c0m1=4 and c1m1=3, where c denotes contradiction and m denotes
missing evidence. Both arms used Astra for execution and repair; A used
the Astra judge and B used Jev Noul.

A delivered 11/11 strict/recovered successes. B delivered 0/11, although
all 11 of its last candidates passed the existing task oracle and had rule
gold `(c,m)=(0,0)`. Jev continued to report `(0,1)` and withheld all 11
deliveries with `external_verification_required`. B consumed the nonfinal
negative feedback in every included run. The observed failure was the
rejection of correct, evidence-supported candidates after repair; it was
not 11 incorrect repaired answers. Candidate correctness does not replace
the native delivery reward or recovery metric.

This stopped sample is descriptive only: no confidence interval,
non-inferiority decision, causal estimate or population generalization is
reported. Four source tasks do not become 11 independent sources. No further
U8n live run is planned; the subsequent evidence-capture code repair does
not alter these retained results. The packet's separate U8n reproduction
recipe recalculates the disclosed candidate diagnoses without model calls.

## Scoring and reproduction boundary

A typed output validator decides response admissibility. Component analyzers
compare retained predictions with fixed labels. An internal verifier returns
a verdict and feedback; GEODE uses them to deliver, request an Astra repair
within the original deadline, or withhold delivery. A separate task-owned
verifier supplies the final task outcome. Internal acceptance does not
establish task success.

In U4/X2, pointwise scores are averaged across forward/reverse orders by
candidate ID before choosing a winner. The selected candidate earns one
pool point if its fixed grade is maximal. Two orderings are not independent
observations. Model format/validation failures remain in the registered
denominator; infrastructure-invalid and unstarted executions remain
unknown/not-measurable.

The public packet provides source/public digest mappings, native-result
projections, row-level grouping, already-unsealed synthetic labels,
frozen analysis/source references and independent verification commands.
Private reasoning, credentials and machine identity are withheld.
Mind2Web test text and candidate bodies must be reacquired from the
upstream pinned source and are not redistributed. Public synthetic test
labels are no longer an unseen holdout for future work.

The −10 pp registered margin was fixed before analysis, but an independent
business-loss or service-SLO derivation was not found. Preserve the registered
decision while assessing practical usefulness separately.
